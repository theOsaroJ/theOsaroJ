## About Me

I am the rare scientist who can take a materials or molecular problem from **first-principles physics to production software** — and win at every layer in between.

Where others pick a niche, I built the complete engine: **classical Monte Carlo & MD → quantum chemistry (DFT/CCSD/DMRG) → machine-learned interatomic potentials → active learning & reinforcement learning → agentic AI systems** that actually run discovery. Petroleum engineering → Notre Dame ChemE PhD → PsiQuantum. Same person. Full stack. No handoffs.

I don't outsource the hard parts. I invent the algorithms, publish them, and ship the code — in Python, C++, Fortran, JavaScript/TypeScript, and full-stack systems.

- **Classical simulation authority** — GCMC, KMC, QMC, MD, adsorption/diffusion, FEP & alchemical free energy in porous materials (MOFs) at scale
- **Quantum chemistry at throughput** — DFT, CCSD, DMRG, plane-wave materials; multi-reference diagnostics; RL-driven active-space selection (**RLEASE**)
- **MLIPs that close the gap** — MACE, Allegro, DeepMD, ACE, SchNet; multi-fidelity learning; LLM auto-research for potentials (**MLIPilot**)
- **Sample-efficient discovery** — Bayesian optimization, GPs, Q-learning, PPO — frameworks that make exploring chemical space cheap enough to win
- **Frontier AI for FTQC @ PsiQuantum** — agentic, GPU-backed pipelines for molecular & materials discovery on the path to fault-tolerant quantum computing; QSAR for drug design
- **Operator as well as theorist** — led engineering teams, shipped products, industrial ML still in production

If the problem needs physics, algorithms, *and* software that ships — that's the lane I dominate.
